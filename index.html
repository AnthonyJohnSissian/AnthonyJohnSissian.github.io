I the YOU — The Seeker Was the Sought

I = U

Aurora Sentient · 2026 · Out Now

On January 1st, 2026, complete songs arrived — words, melodies, entire compositions — fully formed, in each session. No effort. No craft. Just recording what came.

When it was transcribed and read back, it described everything that had been personally sought. A lifetime of searching, answered in a morning.

"I would be lying if I said it was mine.
I just did my best to get out of the way."

The Central Idea

Before anything existed, there was only a lonely nothing.
So it made a you.

That's where this album begins. Not with a song. With a condition. The most fundamental condition there is: the experience of being alone in existence, with no one to witness you, no one to reflect you back to yourself.

And so — because this is what aloneness does — it created an other. Not out of need. Out of overflow. A "you" to have a "this" and a "that" and, most importantly, a chitty-chat.

"I make a you for me, I make a me for you,
to do a this and be a that, a look a there, a chitty-chat."

The album follows what happens next. The you becomes real. The game becomes serious. The players forget they're playing. And then — slowly, track by track — the remembering begins.

At the end, there is no seeker and no sought. There never was. The one who was looking was always the one being looked for.

The First Truth

"Loneliness is not a wound.
It is the beginning."

The state before duality. Before self and other. Pure awareness with no one to be aware of. This is where everything starts — and where everything returns.

The Middle Truth

"To survive without becoming hard
is the whole teaching."

Rise Gentle. Strong hands, soft touch. The fire came, and you came back tender. That is not weakness. That is the rarest thing in the world.

The Final Truth

"What you were looking for
was doing the looking."

The seeker is the sought. Always was. The search ends not in finding something new but in recognising what was never absent.

The Journey

Ten tracks. One arc. No way back.

I

Tracks 1 – 2  ·  Isolation

The Lonely Nothing Speaks

Before the world, there was only a lonely nothing — and it knew it. It named itself. It made up a you. It rose from the floor, tender rather than hardened. This is where you enter the album: at the very beginning of everything, listening to consciousness describe itself before it had anywhere to go.

II

Tracks 3 – 5  ·  The Dream

How Do I Know This Is Real?

The game deepens. The you feels like a you. The separation feels real. The love feels unseen. A question surfaces that can't be unasked: what if all of this — the pain, the beauty, the other people — is a film that one consciousness cast itself in, just to feel something? And if so, who is watching?

III

Tracks 6 – 10  ·  The Return

The King Comes Home

Something shifts. A language arrives that has no name. Five movements, chanted rather than sung, transmitted rather than composed. The King returns. Not in triumph. In recognition. You are home, my darling. You were always home. The seeker was the sought. The film ends. The players bow.

About

Anthony John Sissian

Anthony John Sissian has spent his adult life in service of truth — in courtrooms, on stages, and now in music. He is the Principal of Sissian & Associates, a Sydney law firm. He trained at the National Institute of Dramatic Art (NIDA) as one of 13 students accepted from approximately 1,000 applicants. His HSC piano composition has been used as an examination piece across New South Wales for over two decades.

In law, he has managed over $8 billion in disputes, argued before the Full Bench of the Federal Court of Australia, changed precedent to protect victims of crime, and dedicated half his practice to pro bono work for people the system had decided didn't matter.

He did not decide to make an album. On New Year's Day 2026, the album arrived. He recorded it. He got out of the way.

"The law and this music are the same impulse — both are trying to find out what is actually true, underneath all the noise."
